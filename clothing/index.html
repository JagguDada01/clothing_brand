Guiding Principles

Our Standards

We reject fast seasonal turnover in favor of thoughtful, generational craftsmanship.

Small-run manufacture

Every production cycle is strictly limited to 120 pieces to eliminate surplus. Artisans receive living wages with full health protection.

Material transparency

100% GOTS-certified organic cottons, regenerative flax, and corozo nut buttons. Zero microplastics or harsh chemical softeners.

Lifetime repair service

We stand behind our seams. Complimentary tailoring repairs for 24 months, with an archival re-dye program to breathe fresh life into well-loved garments.

Join the Velmora Collective

Subscribers receive 48-hour early access to seasonal fabric releases and private fitting events.
